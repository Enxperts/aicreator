mission

Teaching kids to think like architects.

We don't teach prompts. We guide every student through the full lifecycle of a real project. The way professional creators work.

Empower students to think as architects of their own ideas. We guide them through the full development lifecycle. From initial spark to a live AI-powered web platform.

★
STEP 01

Spark an idea

Deep search a real problem worth solving. Frame it as a product. Sketch the experience.

⚙
STEP 02

Build the prototype

Create a brand with Leonardo AI. Build a website with Lovable or Webflow. Research with Perplexity.

🚀
STEP 03

Ship a real platform

Deploy a live AI-powered web product. Publish on Lovable, GitHub Pages or Google Firebase. Share it. Be a founder.
